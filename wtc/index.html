Methodology

The model presents major structural systems and typical floors for visual exploration. It is not a set of construction drawings, a complete as-built BIM, or a structural analysis model. Regular floors repeat typical layouts; mechanical floors, sky lobbies, and the hat truss follow grouped report and drawing summaries.

Core columns and local lift cells are calibrated from original Tower A plans; WTC 2 uses a rotated reconstruction. Site outlines use one uniform tower-width scale. Street grades, facade curves, detailed interiors and partial shared basements include documented estimates; floor heights vary. Displayed component dimensions come from this reconstruction; decimal places do not imply surveyed or as-built accuracy. Tower structural north is about 29° clockwise from true north (NCSTAR 1-2B).

Controls

  • Drag with the mouse to rotate the view from your current perspective; the scroll wheel adjusts FOV.
  • WASD moves horizontally in the viewing direction; Q/E moves along world vertical. Click a building component to inspect its available information.
  • Choose buildings, structure layers, cutaways, floors, or camera views in Building Controls.
  • Set a time with the clock field or slider; PLAY animates the day using the DAY CYCLE duration.
  • Presets adjust real-time ray-tracing quality; HDR and FORCE 16:9 affect display.
